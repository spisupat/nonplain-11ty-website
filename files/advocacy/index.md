# Advocacy

Neurodiversity is a fact (the infinite variation in minds), a paradigm (no "right" way for a mind to work) and a movement (civil rights, inclusion & equality for neurodivergent folks). 
Psychology & psychiatry have a long record of ignoring this and causing harms to neurodivergent folks through pathologisation, eugenics, and institutionalisation. Some efforts towards increasing awareness and participatory research:

## Neurodiversity in academia

A workshop at Princeton, co-organised with Mira Nencheva, Laura Murray and Laura Bustamante for the [Princeton University Neurodiversity Collective](https://www.dailyprincetonian.com/article/2023/02/neurodiversity-in-academia-wintersession). History & background of the movement, from critical psychiatry and Mad Pride to neurodiversity; the harms, past and present; where academia is now (legislation, accommodations, stigma, the need to disclose); a panel on lived experience; and breakout rooms on participatory research and teaching. [Slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1CgrrIZO4xmioKPiQTDp-TdSgoumyHsafw5x9swvEvJw/edit?usp=sharing) are here, borrow freely.

## Neurodiversity resource guide for scientists

Written with Charlie Rodriguez-Deliz and the [Scientist Action and Advocacy Network](https://scaan.net) (ScAAN), by neurodivergent scientists for the broader scientific community: what neurodiversity and disability mean, disability rights as civil rights, why this concerns you as a scientist, community-based participatory research, identity-first language, and re-evaluating interventions like ABA. [PDF](https://scaan.net/docs/20211110-neurodiversity_science.pdf).

## #StopTheShock campaign

The Judge Rotenberg Center in Massachusetts still uses electric shock devices on disabled residents. The FDA banned the devices in 2020; a federal court overturned the ban in 2021; Congress restored the FDA's authority in 2022; the FDA re-proposed the ban in 2024 and missed its own deadline to finalise it in 2026. [Campaign page](https://autisticadvocacy.org/stoptheshock/) and [fact sheet](https://autisticadvocacy.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/stop-the-shock-fact-sheet-2024.pdf) from the Autistic Self Advocacy Network.
