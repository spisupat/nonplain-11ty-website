# Anxiety, addiction & continual learning

<p class="meta">Princeton Neuroscience Institute, 2019–2023 &middot; postdoc with Yael Niv</p>

Why do some beliefs and action policies resist updating? In the latent-cause framework, the brain infers which hidden cause is generating its current experience, and a new experience either updates an existing cause or creates a new one. Get that wrong in one direction and a fear never extinguishes; in the other, a drug association survives abstinence and relapse looks like chasing the first high. Both are failures of continual learning.

In the [Niv lab](https://nivlab.princeton.edu/) I built hierarchical Bayesian nonparametric models of human inductive biases for aversive continual learning, to identify where someone's learning has stalled, in order to deliver adaptive and personalised psychotherapy. With Isabel Berwian and colleagues we also wrote up how computational models of learning map onto what CBT actually does (exposure, cognitive restructuring).
