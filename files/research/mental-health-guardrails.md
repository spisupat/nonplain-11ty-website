# Mental health guardrails

<p class="meta">Limbic, 2023–2024</p>

At [Limbic](https://limbic.ai) I was an ML scientist building clinical AI across the mental healthcare pathway, under medical device regulation.

**Scalable oversight.** Fine-tuned evaluator models that achieved ~15% better alignment with humans than GPT-4o, for scalable evaluation and guardrailing of LLM agents in a real-world safety-critical setting. Bandit algorithms to optimise prompts on those evaluation metrics (>29 iterations in <3 months) while minimising user exposure to risk.

**Steerable agents.** LLM agents grounded in clinical expertise (RAG, fine-tuned classifiers, guardrails), preferred 94% of the time over GPT-4 by domain experts, and translated into production in ~3 months with compliance, engineering and product.
