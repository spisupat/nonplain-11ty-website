# Poetry

## sooth & nail

<div class="poem">
<p>hold fast my skin,<br>
contain what’s within me;<br>
don’t open up steaming<br>
on water or whim</p>
<p>something beneath<br>
fast pulsing or breathing,<br>
stuck under a nail bed<br>
of hands or my feet</p>
<p>sitting at home<br>
but home once forgotten,<br>
I peer inside of my being<br>
fibre and bone</p>
<p>structured and dry,<br>
a plan of a plan I see<br>
patiently screaming<br>
this mouth or an eye</p>
<p>something distracts<br>
there in the ground I keep<br>
plastic syringe I must<br>
throw away fast</p>
<p>was it not pain?<br>
no, tenderness at worst<br>
exposed and sweating<br>
but fearless frame</p>
<p>pores open wide<br>
visceral feeling<br>
inviting me gently to<br>
see what’s inside</p>
<p>see sooth, and say<br>
what this partial membrane<br>
holds within and without<br>
not seance but song</p>
<p>clear as the night<br>
watery organs<br>
not full moon, not half dog<br>
but sobering light</p>
<p>two birds, two stones<br>
council of matriarchs<br>
skeleton woman<br>
from dreams long ago</p>
<p>what did she say?<br>
nothing at all at first,<br>
looks of assurance<br>
I’m not going away</p>
<p>and in this dream<br>
not in a dungeon, no<br>
sitting up, I awake<br>
surrogate stream</p>
<p>hold fast my skin,<br>
I know what’s within me;<br>
a channel, a river bed<br>
water and whim.</p>
</div>
<p class="meta">October 2026 &middot; <a href="https://thelemonbot.wordpress.com/2026/10/28/sooth-nail/" rel="noreferrer" target="_blank">thelemonbot &#x2197;</a></p>

## drip.

<div class="poem">
<p>Two drops on a mossy brick<br>
Surrounded by an impossibly vast expanse<br>
The storm won’t let up, a river in spate<br>
Towering blocks raring to fall.</p>
<p>Two drops slither down in synchronous delight<br>
Trying to not lose themselves<br>
In the deluge, down the brick walls<br>
Occasionally stopping on a blade of grass.</p>
<p>Two drops find a rhythm unlikely<br>
Circling, here-a-stopping, there-a-dropping<br>
Following one another down the plunge<br>
Night falls unbeknownst to anyone.</p>
<p>Two drops join the collective din<br>
Of ten billion drops passing through the wind<br>
Soon to swim back up the sky<br>
And form ten billion more.</p>
</div>
<p class="meta">August 2026 &middot; <a href="https://thelemonbot.wordpress.com/2026/08/08/drip/" rel="noreferrer" target="_blank">thelemonbot &#x2197;</a></p>

## Forgotten.

<div class="poem">
<p>Forgotten words I didn’t say<br>
Forgotten, or just yesterday’s<br>
Soon it will be another<br>
Forgotten doubt</p>
<p>Forgotten dreams I didn’t dream<br>
Misremembered, and better still<br>
Missed memories<br>
I dream without</p>
<p>For gathering in my dusty eye<br>
Stormy day within, without<br>
Is there a chance<br>
Of sudden drought</p>
<p>Forgotten birds, they didn’t mind<br>
Flew high, far, free, and light<br>
Let the wind carry<br>
Them away</p>
</div>
<p class="meta">September 2025 &middot; <a href="https://thelemonbot.wordpress.com/2025/09/25/forgotten/" rel="noreferrer" target="_blank">thelemonbot &#x2197;</a></p>

## ephemera.

<div class="poem">
<p>I catch my reflection in the dusty window pane of a passing train,<br>
Preen, vain, window, pain;<br>
Staring, starring, stardust too caught up in the brilliance of its own fast burn;<br>
Mesmerized, vaporized.<br>
A moment of recognition, of self, of identity, and immediately of vanity<br>
Self, selfish. Consciousness? No, how can I be conscious when I am so confident?<br>
I confide in myself; sometimes this makes me feel powerful, sometimes overpowered<br>
Not conscious of myself, not conscious at all, only consummate, luscious, copious<br>
A flick of the hair, like a comet’s tail<br>
A tilt of the hip, like a<br>
Beckoning (come hither)<br>
Echo<br>
The sounds of my own voice<br>
Writhing about, writing about myself<br>
Gotta get it out.<br>
Why is there such a fine line between self love and self loathing?<br>
To be loved by myself is intoxicating, overbearing<br>
Love, self.</p>
</div>
<p class="meta">May 2023 &middot; <a href="https://thelemonbot.wordpress.com/2023/05/23/ephemera/" rel="noreferrer" target="_blank">thelemonbot &#x2197;</a></p>
