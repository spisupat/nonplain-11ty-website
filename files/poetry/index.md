### <a href="https://thelemonbot.wordpress.com/2026/11/19/peel-new-commuter-poem/" rel="noreferrer" target="_blank">peel/new commuter poem</a>

<div class="poem">
<p>Cork and mottle, cork and sheaf<br>
Pale red, white, and bottled beef.<br>
Wrapped like pastry, foreground trees<br>
Gig workers on metal sheets.<br>
Skies cross down on ocean feet<br>
Fried potatoes, boiled reef.<br>
Water current,<br>
Current grief,<br>
Past present and future teeth<br>
Words abundant, concerns brief,<br>
Water fresh, fresh water thief<br>
Flowers, dresses in the pink<br>
Hearts disabled, bodies bleak<br>
Well lit tunnels, well lit streets<br>
Wealthy neighbours, plastic wreaths<br>
Plastic neighbours, shiny seats<br>
Tables empty, clean, and neat.<br>
Clear conditions, airline breeze<br>
Airways blocked by carried wheeze<br>
Whose ending, and whose reprise?<br>
Whose blank page and whose skin seized?</p>
</div>

### <a href="https://thelemonbot.wordpress.com/2026/10/28/sooth-nail/" rel="noreferrer" target="_blank">sooth & nail</a>

<div class="poem">
<p>hold fast my skin,<br>
contain what’s within me;<br>
don’t open up steaming<br>
on water or whim</p>
<p>something beneath<br>
fast pulsing or breathing,<br>
stuck under a nail bed<br>
of hands or my feet</p>
<p>sitting at home<br>
but home once forgotten,<br>
I peer inside of my being<br>
fibre and bone</p>
<p>structured and dry,<br>
a plan of a plan I see<br>
patiently screaming<br>
this mouth or an eye</p>
<p>something distracts<br>
there in the ground I keep<br>
plastic syringe I must<br>
throw away fast</p>
<p>was it not pain?<br>
no, tenderness at worst<br>
exposed and sweating<br>
but fearless frame</p>
<p>pores open wide<br>
visceral feeling<br>
inviting me gently to<br>
see what’s inside</p>
<p>see sooth, and say<br>
what this partial membrane<br>
holds within and without<br>
not seance but song</p>
<p>clear as the night<br>
watery organs<br>
not full moon, not half dog<br>
but sobering light</p>
<p>two birds, two stones<br>
council of matriarchs<br>
skeleton woman<br>
from dreams long ago</p>
<p>what did she say?<br>
nothing at all at first,<br>
looks of assurance<br>
I’m not going away</p>
<p>and in this dream<br>
not in a dungeon, no<br>
sitting up, I awake<br>
surrogate stream</p>
<p>hold fast my skin,<br>
I know what’s within me;<br>
a channel, a river bed<br>
water and whim.</p>
</div>


### <a href="https://thelemonbot.wordpress.com/2023/05/23/ephemera/" rel="noreferrer" target="_blank">ephemera.</a>

<div class="poem">
<p>I catch my reflection in the dusty window pane of a passing train,<br>
Preen, vain, window, pain;<br>
Staring, starring, stardust too caught up in the brilliance of its own fast burn;<br>
Mesmerized, vaporized.<br>
A moment of recognition, of self, of identity, and immediately of vanity<br>
Self, selfish. Consciousness? No, how can I be conscious when I am so confident?<br>
I confide in myself; sometimes this makes me feel powerful, sometimes overpowered<br>
Not conscious of myself, not conscious at all, only consummate, luscious, copious<br>
A flick of the hair, like a comet’s tail<br>
A tilt of the hip, like a<br>
Beckoning (come hither)<br>
Echo<br>
The sounds of my own voice<br>
Writhing about, writing about myself<br>
Gotta get it out.<br>
Why is there such a fine line between self love and self loathing?<br>
To be loved by myself is intoxicating, overbearing<br>
Love, self.</p>
</div>
